Since this repo has carried the name "jackal" through a game and now a blog, it
seems fair to actually explain the animal.

A jackal is a wild canid — a relative of wolves and foxes, but its own distinct
species. There are three main kinds: the **golden jackal** (North/East Africa, the
Middle East, South Asia), and the **black-backed** and **side-striped** jackals
(both native to sub-Saharan Africa).

They're small compared to wolves — around 15–30 lbs — with a slender build, a
pointed muzzle, large upright ears, and tan or grizzled black-and-tan coloring.
They're adaptable scavengers and hunters, usually living in monogamous pairs or
small family groups, and are known for a distinctive yipping howl.

Ancient Egyptians associated jackals with graveyards because of their scavenging
habits — which is why Anubis, the god of the afterlife, is depicted with a
jackal's head.
