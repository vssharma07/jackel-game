# Recent Features and Shifts in the AI Market

The AI market has moved past basic text chat toward autonomous agents, native multimodal workflows, smaller specialized models, and deeper integration into daily software and physical systems.

## 1. Agentic AI and Autonomous Execution
- **Multi-step task automation:** Agents plan sequences, call external tools and APIs, check results, and handle routine customer support, billing, and operational tasks end-to-end.
- **Coding and software creation agents:** Teams use coding agents across repositories and build internal software instead of buying external tools.
- **Scoped permissions and guardrails:** Enterprise deployments rely on bounded access, human sign-off for sensitive actions, and monitoring to prevent unintended changes.

## 2. Native Multimodality, Voice, and Embodied AI
- **Unified multimodal inputs and outputs:** Models process text, images, audio, video, and files natively within single workflows.
- **Real-time voice and media generation:** Low-latency voice systems support live conversations, while creative tools generate richer video, audio, and music.
- **Robotics and embodied reasoning:** Vision-language-action models help robots and connected devices understand physical spaces and carry out multi-step physical tasks.

## 3. Small Models, Token Efficiency, and Edge AI
- **Smaller and task-specific models:** Organizations pair frontier models with compact, fine-tuned models for specialized tasks to cut latency and cost.
- **Token and compute optimization:** Newer model families focus on higher reasoning quality per token and lower inference cost as token spend remains a constraint for some teams.
- **On-device and hybrid inference:** Running models locally on phones, PCs, and edge hardware reduces network lag, trims cloud bills, and keeps sensitive data on-device.

## 4. Deep App Integration and Vertical Workflows
- **Embedded inside everyday tools:** AI features live directly inside workspaces, browsers, code editors, CRMs, and support suites rather than separate chat windows.
- **Codebase and research context:** Tools index full repositories, documents, and notes to give context-aware answers, edits, and synthesis.
- **Industry-specific rollouts:** Specialized workflows target healthcare, finance, manufacturing, software engineering, and scientific research.

## 5. Governance, Evaluation, and AI Sovereignty
- **Reliability and safety evaluations:** Teams test tool calling, grounding, hallucination rates, and security defenses before production rollout.
- **Data residency and compliance:** Regional regulations, audit trails, and sovereign infrastructure shape how and where models are hosted.
